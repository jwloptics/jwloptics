## 📈 Current Goals
I aim to extend the use of nanophotonic structures from hot atoms to cold atoms—specifically strontium atoms trapped in a magneto-optical trap—to achieve higher cooperativity and enter the strong-coupling regime. This advancement could pave the way for new quantum information and precision measurement technologies.
